What runs here

Every seal, answer-sheet fingerprint, grade, Merkle path and check on this page is computed live by quaestor's own Rust code (grading-core), compiled to WebAssembly. Change your answers in chapter 2 and every later chapter is recomputed.

What is simulated

The zero-knowledge proof itself. In quaestor the grading runs inside the SP1 zkVM, which proves it ran correctly. Producing that proof needs a machine with 24 GB or more of memory, so here the proof is drawn, not generated. The checks around it are the real ones.
